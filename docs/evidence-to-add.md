## A consistent case package

Each case should contain a one-sentence brief, source/credit, one strong still, a short outcome clip and a concise process recording. Keep the original MP4: GIFs are browsing previews. Include one rejected result and the concrete rule it taught the system. State what Muwen designed, what AI helped implement, and what was inherited or supplied by collaborators.

Suggested film outline: 0–10 s production problem; 10–25 s input and automatic first pass; 25–50 s creator choice and local revision; 50–70 s output checks; final sentence: expand, iterate or stop, with the observation behind that decision.

## Evidence that must come from real observation

Use a few exploratory creator sessions to capture task completion, assistance, revision work and output quality. Record actual observations; leave unknowns blank. Do not infer time saved from a short demo or turn a synthetic pass into a customer result. Keep sources and the intended quality target constant when comparing workflows.
