This portfolio connects three kinds of evidence: a Blender asset product, interactive tools, and the decisions behind them. They are at different stages. The assessment below separates what can be inspected today from what still needs a task-based demonstration.

5. **Spatial Media Presenter — editable pacing:** [Sequence case](../projects/05-spatial-media-presenter/). Inspect order, transitions and readable holds.

## Fit with the supplied OpenArt Creative Technologist role

The supplied role asks a practitioner to move from a real creative bottleneck through a working prototype, validation and engineering handoff. The review should therefore start with decisions and inspectable behavior. Finished assets support the production story; they are not the whole proposition. This mapping uses the supplied job description, not an assessment from OpenArt.

AI-assisted development and a packaged Skill are useful evidence, but neither establishes a reliable autonomous agent. Model-API orchestration, agent recovery and comparative creator research remain evidence to build, not labels to add to a screenshot.

## Review every new addition against the role

For each new film, interaction or case revision, ask what it proves: a production bottleneck, a design decision, automatic execution, creator authority, local recovery, delivery acceptance, or a validated next step. Link the evidence to that claim and mark what remains untested. A final film strengthens output evidence; it does not by itself prove usability, autonomy or reduced production time.

Model Breakdown includes selected final-output films as well as inspectable application source. Architecture Growth is a separate company Demo-only case and does not disclose a production workflow.

## What to prove next

A bounded Clockwork timing adaptation is now recorded. Prioritize **an unfamiliar creator repeating that task** and **one unassisted Animated Storyboard session**. These extend the strongest existing evidence: production craft and an actual interface iteration. Record the brief, one local correction, the checked output and the decision it changes. Keep the same sources and quality target when comparing with an existing workflow.

For the remaining cases, use the bounded task at the top of each case page. Run a small exploratory study only after the core input-to-output path works. A few successful sessions justify the next experiment; they do not establish broad market demand or production reliability.

Use the [validation record](creator-validation.md) to capture observations. Expand when the core task succeeds at the agreed quality and corrections remain understandable. Iterate when the task matters but discovery, control or recovery fails. Stop or narrow an automation approach when it loses meaning, target identity, construction logic or takes more correction than the manual workflow.
