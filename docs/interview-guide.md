## A deeper conversation

**Production judgment:** Clockwork and Architecture Growth show why geometry, dependencies, construction order and motion readability matter. My contributions and the role of Codex-assisted implementation are stated in each case.

**Prototype decisions:** Storyboard turns separate previews into one editable board. Persistent IDs, source preview, inclusion controls and local replacement preserve the creator's ability to revise a shot without rebuilding the whole composition.

**Definition of done:** successful rendering is only one check. The Tracking repair distinguishes a file that downloads from one whose encoded duration and frame count actually match the request. Target identity remains a separate acceptance criterion.

**Engineering handoff:** inspect the source packages, version/hash manifests, bounded checks and the Storyboard implementation map. These are reviewable handoff materials; they are not evidence of adoption by an engineering team.

## Current evidence boundary

The portfolio contains shipped asset work, company output examples and interactive prototypes at different maturities. It does not claim measured creator time savings, a completed usability study, universal device compatibility, an autonomous production agent or verified model-API orchestration. The [evidence review](review-guide.md) maps the work to the supplied Creative Technologist role and names the next experiment for each case.
