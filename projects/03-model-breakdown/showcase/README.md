Original videos and stills are 2160 × 3840. The animated WebP previews are 500 × 888 and retain all four complete sequences at 30 fps source timing: 11 s, 9.5 s, 11.5 s and 8 s. Identical frames can share a longer display duration; moving passages keep the source cadence. No motion interpolation, speed-up, reversal, generated imagery or replacement geometry was used. The MP4s remain available for playback controls and fullscreen. Every preview frame was decoded and its duration checked. The supplied originals remain unchanged; this is a presentation-media check, not a new app export or usability test.
