## Try one useful task

[Full walkthrough and design decisions](docs/workflow.md). For video tools, inspect the downloaded file as well as the preview: dimensions alone do not establish correct motion, timing or readability.

## Workflow and creator control

The repeatable method applies visual and staging rules. The director determines component meaning, construction order, camera, emphasis and acceptance.

The [workflow notes](docs/workflow.md) distinguish current behavior from proposed automation. **Contribution:** Defining the visual direction, directing storytelling and reviewing continuity at illoca; Codex assists workflow development. Company designs and source models retain their ownership.
